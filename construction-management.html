CAM Reconciliation in Bozeman, MT

Year-end numbers your tenants can follow.

Every building we manage in Bozeman, Belgrade, and the Gallatin Valley gets a full reconciliation of common area maintenance, taxes, and insurance each year. Every charge is tied back to the lease, so recoveries are complete and tenant questions are easy to answer.

Request a consultation

Why it matters

Most reconciliation problems start in the lease, not the ledger

On a NNN or modified gross lease, CAM is where owners quietly lose money and where tenant relationships get strained. Expenses get coded to the wrong pool, pro-rata shares drift after a remeasure or a new lease, caps and base years get applied inconsistently, and gross-up provisions are skipped on partially occupied buildings.

We start from each lease's actual language, build the expense pools and exclusions around it, and produce statements a tenant's accountant can check line by line. When we take over a building from a previous manager, we review prior reconciliations as part of onboarding and correct billing going forward.

Our process

How a reconciliation runs with MVM

  1. Lease abstract

    We abstract every recovery clause: pro-rata share, expense inclusions and exclusions, caps, base years, admin fees, and gross-up terms.

  2. Expense review

    We review the year's general ledger, recode misclassified expenses, and separate capital items from recoverable operating costs.

  3. Reconciliation

    We calculate each tenant's share against estimates paid, applying caps, gross-up, and admin fees exactly as each lease allows.

  4. Owner review

    You see the full reconciliation, the assumptions behind it, and any open issues before anything goes to tenants.

  5. Tenant statements and follow-up

    Tenants receive clear, itemized statements. We answer their questions, collect balances or issue credits, and reset monthly estimates for the new year.

What you get

A reconciliation that holds up

CAM reconciliation is part of full-service management at MVM, not an add-on. It runs alongside monthly reporting, budgeting, and lease administration, so the numbers stay clean all year instead of getting sorted out in March.

Construction Management Supervision in Bozeman, MT

Someone on the owner's side, from budget to close-out.

We coordinate a full range of construction management supervision projects for commercial building owners in Bozeman, Belgrade, and the Gallatin Valley. We build the budget, plan the work, supervise contractors, and close the project out properly, so you know where the money is going at every stage.

Talk to us about your project

Why it matters

Contractors manage the work. Someone needs to manage the contractors.

Construction is where a building's budget is most exposed. Scopes drift, change orders pile up, schedules slip, and without someone tracking every invoice against the contract, owners often don't see the overruns until the project is nearly done.

We act as your representative throughout the project. We have led more than $5M in completed capital projects, and we bring the same discipline to tenant improvements, building renovations, and major system replacements such as roofing, HVAC, and parking lots.

Our process

How we manage a project

  1. Budget development

    We define the scope with you, gather pricing, and build a realistic budget with contingency before any commitments are made.

  2. Pre-construction planning

    We prepare bid packages, compare contractor proposals on equal terms, review contracts, and plan scheduling and access so tenants are disrupted as little as possible.

  3. Construction & contract supervision

    We visit the site, hold contractors to the scope and schedule in their contracts, and review every change order before it reaches you for approval.

  4. Budget management

    We track every invoice and change order against the budget and give you regular updates on costs committed, costs paid, and what remains.

  5. Project close-out

    We manage the punch list, collect lien waivers, warranties, and as-built documents, and confirm final payments match the work delivered.

What you get

A project that finishes the way it was planned

Construction management supervision is available for buildings we manage and as part of bringing a new building online, so the person who oversaw the work knows the building afterward.

Switching managers and onboarding

How does switching from my current property manager work?

Start by checking the termination and notice terms in your current management agreement. Once notice is given, we coordinate the handoff directly with the outgoing manager: leases and amendments, tenant ledgers, security deposits, vendor contracts, keys and access, and prior-year CAM reconciliations. Tenants receive a notice explaining where to pay rent and how to reach us, so the change is smooth on their end.

What happens during onboarding?

We abstract every lease, set the building up in our accounting system, review vendor contracts and insurance, confirm CAM budgets and estimates, and walk the property to document its condition. If we find billing errors from prior years, we bring them to you with a plan to correct them.

New Building Onboarding in Bozeman, MT

From certificate of occupancy to a building that runs itself.

The first year of a new commercial building sets the pattern for every year after it. We take new office, retail, and industrial buildings in Bozeman, Belgrade, and the Gallatin Valley from delivery to fully operational, with contracts, budgets, tenants, and warranties set up correctly from day one.

Talk to us before delivery

Why it matters

The expensive mistakes happen in year one

New buildings come with a long list of first-time decisions: which vendors to hire, what the first CAM budget should be, how utilities get split between tenants, and who tracks the dozens of equipment warranties that start running the day the building is delivered. When those decisions are rushed, owners pay for it later through underestimated budgets, service contracts that were never bid, tenants billed on the wrong basis, and warranty claims that expire before anyone files them.

We have taken brand-new commercial buildings through this process before. Bringing us in before delivery means the building opens with its systems, contracts, and accounting already in place.

Our process

How we bring a new building online

  1. Before delivery

    We review leases and plans with you, build the first-year operating and CAM budgets, and outline the FF&E budget for common areas so estimates are realistic from the first invoice.

  2. Service contracts

    We competitively bid janitorial, snow removal, landscaping, waste, HVAC maintenance, fire and life safety, and other recurring services, then set up vendors with clear scopes and insurance on file.

  3. Utilities and metering

    We transfer utilities, confirm how each is metered, and set up any submetering or allocation method so tenants are billed exactly as their leases require.

  4. Warranty and closeout tracking

    We collect closeout documents and O&M manuals from the contractor, log every system warranty with its start and end dates, and manage punch list items and warranty claims.

  5. Tenant onboarding

    We coordinate move-ins, set each tenant up in the portal for rent payment and maintenance requests, and walk tenants through building procedures and contacts.

  6. First-year review

    At the end of the first year we compare actual costs to budget, complete the first CAM reconciliation, and adjust estimates and contracts based on how the building really runs.

What you get

A building set up the right way

New building onboarding leads directly into full-service management with MVM, so the person who set up your building is the one who keeps running it.
